Study in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is the perfect destination to learn the Arabic language and Islamic culture. It is unique with historic places and contrasts in scenery. At the Arabic Language School, students learn by reciting the Holy Qur'an and using expressions used by local people. During their stay, students live with a family to experience the culture and share accommodations.

Exploring Abha City

Abha is the capital of Asir province, located 2,200 meters above sea level. Its mild climate makes it a popular tourist spot. Abha is famous for its traditional mud-brick houses and its rich heritage. The town has a buzzing marketplace where you can find regional crafts. Visitors enjoy the atmosphere at local summer festivals, and some try paragliding! With a population of over 250,000, Abha's popularity continues to grow today.
