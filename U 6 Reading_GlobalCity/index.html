Reading Passage 1 · نص القراءة ١
Jeddah: Global City
جدة: مدينة عالمية
🎵 Playback Speed: 1.0x
0.5x 2.0x
🔊 Reading aloud... (0/0) 00:00
👆 Touch or hover any word for its Arabic translation. المسّ أي كلمة لرؤية ترجمتها

For 20 consecutive years, Jeddah has been named a "global city". It offers a high quality of life to its citizens. Residents point out several aspects such as cultural diversity, rich cultural heritage, and high quality of education.

Jeddah is a leading destination for international students and pilgrims. It's the gateway to Makkah. Jeddah has been an example of innovation. Landmarks include King Fahd's Fountain, which is the highest fountain in the world. Plans exist to build the Kingdom Tower, which will be the world's tallest building.

During the 1980s, Jeddah became one of the largest open-air art galleries. Modern sculptures, ranging from abstract works to traditional coffee pots, decorate the streets. Bayt Naseef in the historic center is a charming blend of old houses and skyscrapers. The marketplace features boutiques and souqs.

The city is famous for recreation and athletic clubs. A light breeze cools the coast where visitors enjoy coral reefs and scuba divers. Jeddah is truly the "Bride of the Red Sea."

A: Japan

Japan's public transportation is efficient. Its bullet trains can travel at 186 miles per hour and are extremely punctual. Fares for trains are high; they are as expensive as airplane fares. However, the time is shorter than going to an airport.

B: Hong Kong

Hong Kong is among the safest cities. The overall crime rate is roughly the same as in Singapore, and lower than Tokyo or Toronto.

C: Cambridge

Home to the oldest universities. Nowadays, it's not as quiet, but quality of life is high. A quarter of the population cycles through green areas, over bridges, through parks, and along narrow streets.

D: Mexico City

Surveys show the city has the worst traffic, even worse than Sao Paulo. The average commuter spends four hours daily in traffic in Mexico City. Approximately 6 million vehicles like cars and taxis are carrying millions of people.
