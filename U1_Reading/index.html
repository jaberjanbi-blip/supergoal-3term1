Reading Passage · نص القراءة
Do College Students Have a Healthy Lifestyle?
هل يتبع طلاب الجامعات أسلوب حياة صحي؟
🔊 Reading aloud... (0/0) 00:00
Speed: 0.9×
👆 Touch or hover any word to see its Arabic translation instantly.  |  المسّ أي كلمة لترى ترجمتها فوراً

The majority of college students seem to be healthy because they are still young. However, that doesn't mean they shouldn't try to improve their lifestyle and daily routine. We carried out research at a local college to find the truth about students' habits.

Our findings show that the average student diet differs greatly from a balanced one. While they know eating fresh produce has a clear benefit, most students eat mainly rice and fried food.

Getting enough sleep is another important factor. Studies have shown that it has a positive effect on memory and concentration. On average, however, most of them only get about six hours of sleep every night.

Finally, students who exercise for at least 30 minutes a day feel more energetic. Healthy choices help them succeed, so they must manage their time well.
