🩸 THE GIFT OF LIFE Trent Olsen donated blood for the 100th time on June 23rd. He made his first donation in his 20s. Trent and his colleagues decided to help when they saw a mobile clinic. He visits Fairview Clinic regularly; one donation can save up to three lives.

🏆 ATHLETE OF THE YEAR Ahmed Jamal was awarded "Athlete of the Year" for his outstanding leadership at Al Marwah High School. Ahmed was born with a crippling disease that made it difficult to walk. This did not stop him from playing football. He formed a team and now competes in the Olympics Junior League. When he was 12, his older brother, Ali, taught him to play in the park. Now, he is an example for all.

🏖 BLUE FLAG FOR SUNSET Over 100 employees from local hotels gathered at Sunset Beach for a clean-up operation. They combed the beach for bottles and litter. The owners took responsibility for keeping the beach clean, and the Foundation awarded them the Blue Flag for their success.

👶 NEWBORN David and Mary Ann Taylor are the proud parents of newborn twins, Linda and Jenny. The infants were born at Newton Maternity Hospital on June 21st. Both the mother and babies are in good health.

The Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques King Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud was born in Riyadh on December 31, 1935. He was educated at the Princes' School in Riyadh where he studied sciences, religion, and the Holy Qur'an. In 2012, he was appointed Crown Prince by his predecessor, King Abdullah. King Salman helped Riyadh develop from a mid-sized town into a major urban metropolis. He attracted tourism, business, and investment to the Kingdom through his vision for a strong future.

In his capacity as a prince, he worked with many humanitarian groups that offer relief from natural disasters in the Kingdom and abroad. He has always been known for his dedication to helping others in global need. Through his work, he supported many cultural and royal projects and received many awards. He was also Chairman of the Riyadh Charity for Sciences and President of the Prize for the Memorization of the Holy Qur'an.

King Salman holds many degrees and academic honors from various international universities. He was awarded an honorary doctorate in literature from the University of Umm Al-Qura in Makkah. Today, his leadership continues to guide the Kingdom toward success and prosperity in every sector.
