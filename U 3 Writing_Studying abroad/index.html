Step 4: Paragraph Structure
الخطوة الرابعة: بنية الفقرة
📌 A good paragraph is built from three parts. Learn each one, then practise finding them.
تتكوّن الفقرة الجيدة من ثلاثة أجزاء. تعلّم كل جزء ثم تدرّب على إيجاده.
Part الجزءWhat It DoesFrom the Paragraph
🔵 Topic Sentence
الجملة الرئيسية
States the main idea
تحدد الفكرة الرئيسية
Studying in a new country is a great experience.
🟢 Supporting Sentences
الجمل الداعمة
Give details and examples
تعطي تفاصيل وأمثلة
You can learn… It helps you grow… It can even help…
🟣 Concluding Sentence
جملة الخاتمة
Gives a solution or summary
تقدم حلاً أو خاتمة
I think everyone should try living in a new place once!

💡 Writing Tips

• Start your topic sentence with a capital letter.
• Use simple present tense and modals like "can" for general truths.
• End every sentence with a full stop.
• Try to use at least 2 lesson words.
