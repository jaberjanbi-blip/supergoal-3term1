Fahad is walking in the park, and he meets a friend, Saeed.
Saeed: Hi, Fahad. You're looking good! Do you work out every day?
Fahad: Yes. I have changed my lifestyle completely. Since I had my heart problem, I've walked every morning, I've eaten healthier food, and I haven't stayed out late at night the way I used to. And I've stopped smoking. I've tried to have a healthier lifestyle.
Saeed: And are you still working in the airport control tower? It's been three years since I worked there with you.
Fahad: No, I've had a new job for six months. I'm training air traffic controllers. It's less stressful, and I have more time off. What about you?
Saeed: Well, I got married. I've been married for almost a year. My wife and I like to cook, and I've gotten a desk job. So, I've gained a bit of weight. That's why I've started to exercise.
