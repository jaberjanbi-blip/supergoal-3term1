Step 4: Paragraph Structure

📌 A good paragraph is built from three parts. Learn each one, then practise finding them.

Part الجزءWhat It DoesFrom the Paragraph
🔵 Topic Sentence
الجملة الرئيسية
States the main idea
تحدد الفكرة الرئيسية
My computer is my favorite possession.
🟢 Supporting Sentences
الجمل الداعمة
Give details and examples
تعطي تفاصيل وأمثلة
My father gave it… I have used it… It has…
🟣 Concluding Sentence
جملة الخاتمة
Gives a solution or summary
تقدم حلاً أو خاتمة
I use it at my table for three hours daily, and it is very helpful.

💡 Writing Tips

• Start your topic sentence with a capital letter.
• Use present perfect with "since" or "for" to say how long you've had it.
• End every sentence with a full stop.
• Try to use at least 2 lesson words.
